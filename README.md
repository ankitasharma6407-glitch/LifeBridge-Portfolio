# LifeBridge – Connecting Crisis to Care

LifeBridge is an AR/VR-based emergency-response prototype developed by **Team Infinity** from **Patna Women’s College** for the **Young Innovators STEM Lab (YISL) Workshop – Cohort 1**.

## Problem Statement

Road accidents often require immediate first aid, but professional medical help may take time to arrive. People present at the accident site may not know the correct emergency steps, which can lead to delayed or improper assistance.

LifeBridge aims to reduce this critical gap by providing AR-based real-time first-aid guidance and VR-based emergency-response training.

## Prototype Description

LifeBridge uses:

- **AR (Augmented Reality)** to provide quick, step-by-step first-aid guidance during an emergency.
- **VR (Virtual Reality)** to provide a simulated environment where users can practice emergency-response situations safely.

The prototype includes guidance for:
- Scene safety
- Victim responsiveness
- Calling emergency help
- Breathing check
- Bleeding control
- CPR guidance

The aim of LifeBridge is not to replace professional medical care, but to help people use the critical waiting time effectively until professional help arrives.
